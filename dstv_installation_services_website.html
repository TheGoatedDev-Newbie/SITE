Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a quote?

Simply click any of our WhatsApp buttons or fill in the booking form on this site. We provide instant free estimates based on your specific needs.

What causes error code E48-32?

E48-32 means your decoder is receiving no signal. This can be caused by strong wind shifting your dish, a damaged LNB, loose cables, or extreme bad weather.

Do you work on weekends?

Yes! Our technicians operate 7 days a week, including Saturdays, Sundays, and public holidays.
